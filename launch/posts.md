## LinkedIn (founder/student voice)
I forgot every word I looked up during placement prep. So I built Gengo.

It's a free, no-account vocabulary trainer that runs in the browser:
• look a word up → meaning, IPA, example, synonyms
• hear it in a clear voice, then say it back
• quiz rounds built only from the words you saved
• 5-minute Focus Sprints that master words and protect your streak

Built with plain HTML/CSS/JS and free public APIs. Phone, tablet, laptop.
Made for final-year students prepping for aptitude tests and HR rounds — if that's you or your juniors, it's here: https://praneet-admin.github.io/gengo/
