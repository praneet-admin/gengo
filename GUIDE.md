# 🐰 Gengo — The Complete Guide

**Gengo** is a free web app that helps you build a personal English vocabulary in short daily sessions — look up a word, hear it, say it, translate it, see it in real writing, quiz yourself, and keep a streak going. It runs in any browser on a laptop, tablet or phone, needs no account, and stores everything on your device.

👉 **Open it:** https://praneet-admin.github.io/gengo/

Your guide is **Gen**, the purple rabbit with the Gengo carrot. He greets you, cheers you on, sulks a little when you get one wrong, and keeps you company during focus sprints.

## 1. What Gengo can do — at a glance

| You want to… | Go to | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Understand a new word | **Learn** | meaning, pronunciation (IPA + audio), part of speech, example sentence, synonyms, antonyms |
| Know it in another language | **Translate** | Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Hindi, Tamil, Japanese — spoken aloud too |
| Pronounce it properly | **Speak** | a clear female or male voice reads it; you say it back and get a match score |
| See how real writers use it | **Context** | short Wikipedia excerpts with the word highlighted |
| Check what stuck | **Quiz** | rounds of 5 multiple-choice questions from your own saved words |
| Keep everything in one place | **My Words** | your collection with mastery stars, filter, listen, remove |
| Build a habit | **Focus Sprint · Today** | a 5-minute distraction-free sprint, daily goal, streaks, XP, levels, Streak Shields |

Everything works on phone, tablet and laptop; nothing needs a login.

## 2. Getting started in 60 seconds

The first time you open Gengo, **Gen offers a 30-second tour** — six spotlight steps that point out the search box, saving, the six sections, the Focus Sprint, your daily goal and the header stats. The tour also covers the accessibility switches. Take it or skip it; you can get **instructions any time** from the yellow **Help** button in the header (laptop) or **☰ → How to use Gengo** (phone/tablet) — that dialog also offers *Take the guided tour* and *Keyboard shortcuts*.

1. **Open** https://praneet-admin.github.io/gengo/ — you land on **Learn**.
2. **Tap a suggestion chip** (Resilient, Eloquent, Curious…) or type any English word and press **Explore**.
3. Read the card. Press **Listen** 🔊 to hear it.
4. Press **Save Word (+10 XP)**. Confetti — that's your first activity of the day and your first 10 XP.
5. Do two more activities (save another word, run a quiz round, or finish a Focus Sprint) and today's goal is done: **3/3**, and your streak begins 🔥.

That's the whole loop. Five minutes a day is enough.

## 4. Learn — look up a word

**Search** any English word (up to 40 characters) and press **Explore** or Enter. Six suggestion chips get you started.

**The word card shows**
- The **headword** with its **IPA pronunciation** (e.g. /rɪˈzɪliənt/) and **part of speech** (noun, adjective…).
- Where the entry came from (Gengo uses free dictionaries: dictionaryapi.dev, Wiktionary and Datamuse).
- **Meaning**, an **Example** sentence, **Synonyms** and **Antonyms** as chips — tap a synonym to look it up instantly.
- **Listen** buttons for the word, the meaning and the example.

**Save Word (+10 XP)** adds it to My Words and counts as one activity. It turns into "✓ Saved · Remove" once saved.

**Carry the word onward** with the links at the bottom: *Translate →*, *Say it →*, *In context →*.

If a word isn't found, Gengo tells you plainly and offers **Try again** or a quiz instead.

---

## 5. Translate — in 8 languages

1. The box is **pre-filled with your current word**. Use the chips to swap in **its definition** or **its example**, or type anything up to 600 characters.
2. **Pick a language card**: Hola · Spanish, Bonjour · French, Hallo · German, Ciao · Italian, Olá · Portuguese, नमस्ते · Hindi, வணக்கம் · Tamil, こんにちは · Japanese. (A dropdown below does the same.)
3. Press **Translate**.

The result shows English on the left and the translation on the right with a **Listen** button that speaks it in that language's voice.

Translation uses the free MyMemory service — no key, no setup. It has a daily fair-use quota; if you hit it, Gengo says so.

## 6. Speak — hear it, say it

Speak needs a current word — look one up in Learn first.

### Listening
- Choose a voice: **Female** (default) or **Male**. On a Mac these are Karen and Daniel, the two clearest built-in voices; on other devices Gengo picks the best available English voice, and hides the switch if there's only one.
- **Play word · Play definition · Play example · Stop**.
- **Listen again = slow.** Like Google Translate: press any Listen/Play button a second time on the same text and Gengo plays it slowly (about half speed) so every syllable is clear — the button shows a 🐢 once slow mode is armed. Every further replay stays slow; a different word starts at normal speed again. Works for words, meanings, examples and translations.
- **✨ Natural voices (recommended).** In Speak → Listening, press **Download natural voices** once (≈90 MB, kept in your browser). From then on every English Listen/Play button uses a clear, human-sounding on-device voice — **Heart** (female) or **Michael** (male) — in any browser, even offline. Translations still use your device's voice for that language. Switch it off any time with the checkbox.
- Without the download, Gengo picks the most natural voice your browser has: Microsoft *Natural* voices in Edge, Google voices in Chrome on Windows/Android, Ava/Karen and Daniel on a Mac. Female is the default; Male is one tap away.

### Speaking practice
1. Choose what to read aloud: **the word** or **the example sentence**.
2. Press **🎤 Start speaking** and allow microphone access the first time.
3. Read the text. Gengo shows **"We heard …"**, a **match meter** (0–100 %) and a short verdict.

**How recognition works**: Gengo first uses your browser's own recogniser (Chrome, Edge, Safari). If that's blocked on your network, it automatically switches to an **on-device recogniser** (Whisper, downloaded once, ~40 MB) — your audio never leaves the browser. There's also always **"Or type what you hear"**: play the word, then spell it from memory and press **Check**.

> The match score compares words, not accent — it's a practice aid, not a pronunciation exam.

## 7. Context — the word in the wild

Context searches **Wikipedia** for your current word (or anything you type) and shows up to three articles: thumbnail, title, an excerpt with your word **highlighted**, and a "Read on Wikipedia ↗" link. It's the quickest way to see how a word behaves in real sentences.

## 8. Quiz — test yourself

The quiz **unlocks once you've saved 4 words** (it needs wrong answers to choose from).

- Each **round has 5 questions**. A definition appears in a yellow bubble; pick the matching word from **A–D**.
- **Correct** → mint glow, **+5 XP**, and a **combo counter** (×2, ×3…) for consecutive hits. **Wrong** → the right answer is revealed. Either way, press **Continue**.
- After question 5: **Round complete!** with three tiles — total XP, accuracy, time — and Gen celebrating.
- Keys **1–4** or **A–D** answer, **Enter** continues.

Gengo asks about words you haven't been asked about recently, and each word only pays XP once per day, so you learn instead of farming points.

---

## 9. My Words — your collection

Every saved word lives here, newest first.

- Header: "**4 words saved · 1 mastered**".
- **Filter** by word or meaning.
- Each card: word, IPA, part of speech, **mastery stars ★★☆** (see [section 10](#10-focus-sprint--five-minutes-no-distractions)), meaning, saved date, example, **Listen**, **Remove**.
- **Clear All** wipes the collection after a confirmation — your XP and streak are untouched.

## 10. Focus Sprint — five minutes, no distractions

The sprint is Gengo's *deep-work* mode, and the only place two things happen: words get **mastered**, and you earn **Streak Shields**.

### Starting
Press **Start** on the Focus Sprint card. The whole screen switches to the **focus screen** (and the browser goes fullscreen where it can): Gen, a big **05:00** ring, three strike dots, and your **flashcard drill**.

### The flashcard drill — "Master your words"
- A card shows one of your saved words with its **★ mastery**. **Tap it** (or press Space) to flip to the meaning and example.
- **Got it** → the word gains one mastery star (one step per word per sprint) and you earn **+2 XP** (up to 20 XP per sprint).
- **Again** → the card goes back into the deck to come around later.
- Keys: **→** Got it, **←** Again.
- Three stars = **mastered** — shown in My Words.

### The rules
- **Leaving the tab or app pauses the sprint and costs a strike** (Gen turns sad, a toast tells you). You have **3 strikes**; the third **cancels the sprint** — no XP, no shield.
- The first 3 seconds after Start are a grace period, and pressing Esc to leave fullscreen is *not* a strike — the focus screen stays.
- **Pause** stops the clock without a strike. **Resume sprint** continues. **Give up** ends it with no reward.
- Closing the tab mid-sprint makes the browser ask if you're sure.

### Finishing
When the ring completes: confetti, **+15 XP**, the number of cards you reviewed, one activity towards your daily goal, and **🛡 a Streak Shield** (if you have fewer than two).

## 11. XP, levels, streaks & shields

### XP
| Action | XP |
|---|---|
| Save a word | +10 |
| Correct quiz answer | +5 (once per word per day) |
| Finish a Focus Sprint | +15 |
| "Got it" on a flashcard during a sprint | +2 (max 20 per sprint) |

### Levels
XP adds up to levels: Level 2 at 50 XP, then 120, 220, 350, 500, 700, 950, 1 250 and Level 10 at 1 600 XP. The level bar on the Today card shows how far to the next one.

### Daily goal & streak
- **3 activities a day** complete the goal (saves, quiz rounds *or* sprints all count). The goal path fills 1 → 2 → ★, then Gen celebrates and a **Daily recap** shows your XP, saves, quiz score and sprints.
- Do at least one activity a day and your **streak** 🔥 grows. Milestones at **3, 7, 14, 30, 60 and 100 days** get a special cheer. Your **best streak** is remembered.
- The **7-day strip** on the Today card shows how much you did each day.

### Streak Shields 🛡
- Finishing a Focus Sprint banks a shield (hold up to **2**).
- Miss a day and, on your next visit, a shield is **spent automatically** to cover it — the day shows 🛡 in the strip and your streak survives.
- The Today card always tells you how many shields you hold.

## 13. Display & accessibility settings

On a laptop the header has five switches (on phones the app is already tuned, so they're hidden):

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| **✦ Motion** | turns off all animations, confetti and the floating background |
| **◐ Contrast** | high-contrast colours and thicker borders |
| **A Text** | cycles text size: normal → large → larger |
| **Aa Easy read** | wider letter/line spacing and a plain font — helpful for dyslexia |
| **⌨ Keys** | shows the keyboard-shortcut sheet |

Gengo also respects your **system settings** (reduced motion, Windows High Contrast). Every control works with a keyboard and a screen reader; nothing is audio-only; touch targets are at least 44 px.

## 15. Your data & privacy

- **No account, no server.** Everything — saved words, XP, streak, shields, settings — is stored in your browser (`localStorage`, key `gengo-v1`) on *this* device. Clear your browser data and it's gone; use another browser or device and you start fresh.
- Word lookups go to free public dictionaries, translations to MyMemory, context searches to Wikipedia. Only the text you search/translate is sent — never your collection or progress.
- Speaking practice uses your browser's recogniser or an on-device model; with the on-device model, audio never leaves your browser.
- **Reset everything**: open the browser console and run `Gengo.resetState()`, or clear site data for the page.

## 16. Tips for learning faster

1. **Save words you actually meet** — from articles, shows, work — not just the suggestions.
2. **Run the full loop on each word**: Learn → Say it → Translate → In context. Four encounters beat one.
3. **Quiz daily.** Recognition in a quiz is what makes a word yours.
4. **One Focus Sprint a day** masters words *and* insures your streak with a shield.
5. **Mark "Again" honestly.** A word you fumble should come back.
6. **Use the example sentence in Speak** — reading whole sentences trains rhythm, not just single sounds.
7. Chase the milestones: 3 days is a habit, 7 is a week, 30 makes you a regular.

## 17. Troubleshooting & FAQ

**A word search takes a while / says the dictionary is slow.**
Gengo races two dictionary sources and shows whichever answers first (usually within 3 seconds). If both fail, check your connection and press Try again.

**Translate says the daily quota is used up.**
MyMemory's free quota resets daily. Try again tomorrow — everything else keeps working.

**"Start speaking" says recognition isn't available.**
Gengo will offer the on-device recogniser (allow the one-time download) or **type what you hear**. Make sure the mic permission is allowed for the site.

**The voice sounds robotic / male and female sound the same.**
Your device may have only one English voice installed. On Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Manage Voices. On Windows: Settings → Time & Language → Speech → Manage voices.

**My sprint paused by itself.**
You (or a notification) switched away from the tab — that's a strike. Come back and press **Resume sprint**. Three strikes cancel the sprint.

**The Quiz says it's locked.**
Save at least 4 words first.

**I see an old version of the app.**
Hard-refresh the page (Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + R).

**Does it work offline?**
Gengo needs the internet to load and for lookups, translation, context and browser speech recognition. Once the page is open, saving words, the quiz and the Focus Sprint keep working if the connection drops.

**Is anything sent to Gengo's makers?**
No. There is no backend and no analytics.

## 18. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Activity** | anything that counts towards the daily goal: saving a word, a correct quiz answer, finishing a sprint |
| **Daily goal** | 3 activities a day |
| **Streak** | consecutive days with at least one activity |
| **Streak Shield 🛡** | earned by finishing a sprint; automatically covers one missed day |
| **XP / Level** | experience points and the level they add up to |
| **Mastery ★** | a word's 0–3 star rating, raised only by "Got it" during Focus Sprints |
| **Focus Sprint** | 5-minute timed session with the flashcard drill and a 3-strike leave rule |
| **Strike** | leaving the tab/app during a sprint; three cancel it |
| **Combo** | consecutive correct quiz answers in a round |
| **Gen** | the rabbit. He's on your side. |
